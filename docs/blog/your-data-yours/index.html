About the app

Your data stays yours

Everything Untitled keeps is in a folder you own, in formats nothing locks.

  $ ls ~/untitled
    profiles/     your agent's settings, plain config
    memories/     what it remembers about you, plain text
    vault/        your keys and logins, encrypted
    sessions/     every conversation, plain text

  copy the folder → done. that's the whole export.

No proprietary database, no export button that emails you a zip in three days. Your conversations are markdown. Your settings are config files. Your memories are plain text you can open in any editor right now.

This is deliberate. An agent that watches your work is only trustworthy if leaving is free. If the exit costs you your history, the product owns you, no matter what the landing page says.

Delete the folder and Untitled never existed. Keep it and you can run the same data on the open-source engine yourself, forever. We think that's what "your data" should mean.
